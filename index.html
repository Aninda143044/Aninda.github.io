Overview

I am Aninda Bhattacharjee, currently pursuing my Master of Science in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Aizu (Japan) under the supervision of Professor Truong Cong Thang.

I also serve as an Assistant Director at the National Cyber Security Agency, ICT Division, People’s Republic of Bangladesh. In this capacity, I specialize in the development and analysis of cybersecurity legal frameworks, regulatory policies, cross-platform social listening, quantitative content analysis, and the promotion of socio-technical awareness regarding cybersecurity best practices and digital hygiene.

worked as a remote Research Assistant with Dr. Jaydeb Sarker at the University of Nebraska, Omaha, focusing on bug report analysis in the field of software engineering and the explainability of Generative AI.

Previously, I served as an Assistant Programmer in the Department of ICT, ICT Division, where I oversaw the monitoring of 2,000 digital labs across secondary and higher secondary schools and developed a standardized Python programming curriculum and instructional manual for 27,000 higher secondary students in Bangladesh.

I earned my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science & Engineering from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET) (Jan. 2015 – Aug. 2019) with a CGPA of 3.19/4.00, completing my undergraduate thesis on Comparative Analysis between K-Means Clustering and K-Medoid Clustering.
